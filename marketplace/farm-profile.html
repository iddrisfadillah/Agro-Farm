Our Story

Green Valley Orchards has been growing heirloom vegetables and fruit on the outskirts of Kumasi since 2008. What started as a two-acre family plot has grown into a 40-acre certified organic operation, still run by the same family and the same commitment to flavor-first farming.

We believe produce tastes better when it's grown with patience — no synthetic pesticides, no rushed harvests, just healthy soil and traditional techniques passed down over three generations.
